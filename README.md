## Why Resumio

- **Free to use** — No subscription, payment details, or credit card required.
- **No sign-up** — Start building without an account or email address.
- **Build multiple resumes** — Keep tailored versions for different roles in one library.
- **Write with a live preview** — Organize your details across ten focused sections and see the A4 layout update as you edit.
- **Download a real PDF** — Export selectable, searchable text with clickable links.
- **Your resumes stay in your browser** — No account or cloud sync; your resume library remains on this browser and device.
